FAQs & Policies - Parking Services

General Information

Where is Parking and Transportation Services Located?
Parking Services is located at 1332 Jenkins Ave. in the Jenkins Avenue Parking Facility (JAPF), directly south of Dunham and Headington Residential Colleges and next to the Sarkeys Fitness Center. The office is on the southwest corner at ground level inside the garage just before the staircase. Office hours are 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Monday through Friday.

Where can I review parking regulations?
A PDF copy of the Norman Campus Parking and Traffic Regulations booklet is available online.

I have a new car or new license plate. How do I update my Parking Services account?
Manage vehicle information online by logging into your account with your OU Net ID and password, then select "More" to add a vehicle. The system does not allow license plate changes—if you have a new plate, re-add as a new vehicle. You cannot delete vehicles; contact the parking office with proof of sale to remove sold vehicles.

Where can I park in metered parking for free?
Parking meters require payment 24 hours daily. Exceptions include vehicles with valid OU permits from 5 p.m. to 2 a.m., or those displaying both a valid OU permit and state-issued disability placard.

Do disability placard-holders have to pay a meter if they park at one?
No. A valid OU permit and either a state-issued disability placard or temporary six-week OU disability permit are required, displayed on the rearview mirror or dashboard, clearly visible from outside.

Can I donate to the food pantry instead of paying the citation?
Yes, with conditions: student must appeal within 14 days; citation must be $50 or less; maximum two citations per academic year; donation must equal at least 80% of citation cost prior to appeal submission; proof from Food Pantry required.

Parking Permits

Can I order my permit online?
Yes, except for Graduate Assistant, Vendor, and Short-term permits. Pick up from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. weekdays at 1332 Jenkins Ave., or allow 7-10 business days for mailing. All vehicles must have permits properly displayed; proof of purchase is insufficient.

What is the cost of a permit?
Student permits (Commuter, Housing, Commuter Law): $288 annually (Aug. 15–June 15); Evening: $97; Faculty/staff: $326 (Aug. 15–Aug. 15); Motorcycle: $53; Reserved parking (Elm/Asp): $1,245.

Can I get a refund for my permit?
Yes. Full refund if returned within first two weeks of fall semester; after that, prorated refunds for unused portions.

Can I purchase a permit for just one semester?
No. Prices are prorated weekly. For second semester only starting Jan. 1st, approximately $155 for commuter/housing permits, valid until June 15.

How can I pay for a parking permit?
Students may purchase online and pay via Bursar's account or credit card. Faculty/staff may pay by credit card or payroll deduction online. Others may pay cash, personal check, or credit card at the office.

May I use my permit in another vehicle?
Yes. Hang tags transfer between vehicles but not people. Unauthorized lot use holds the permit owner responsible for citations.

How many parking permits are issued each year?
Approximately 4,211 faculty/staff; 201 graduate assistants; 10,726 students.

What do I do if my parking permit is lost or stolen?
Report immediately to Parking Services. Replacement costs $15.

Parking Spaces and Garages

Where can visitors park on campus?
Visitors with permits may park in permit-required lots (faculty/staff, commuter, etc.), but not in reserved or metered spaces.

How many parking spaces are on campus?
Approximately 15,000 spaces on Norman campus (subject to change due to construction).

How many parking spaces are designated for students?
Approximately 10,000 spaces designated for student parking.

Where is motorcycle parking?
Motorcycle parking throughout campus is marked on the parking map with motorcycle symbols. Motorcycles must use designated spaces only, not full-vehicle spaces.

I have a permit but I can't find a place to park. What do I do?
Use the Duck Pond lot (MP4 on map), a large multi-purpose lot east of the football stadium with available spaces.

If I don't have a permit, where can I park on campus?
Park permit-free on the north side of Lloyd Noble Center and use CART bus shuttle to campus. Non-stop service runs every 5-10 minutes from 7 a.m. to 6 p.m., and every 20 minutes from 6-9 p.m. (reduced summer service).

How many parking garages are on campus?
Three operated by Parking Services: Elm Avenue (576 spaces), Asp Avenue (724 spaces), Jenkins Avenue (1,221 spaces). Union Parking Center operates separately.

Is there hourly parking on campus?
Asp Avenue and Elm Avenue facilities have pay stations on the first ramp for hourly use. Union Parking Center also provides hourly parking.

Parking Citations

How do I appeal a citation?
Appeals must be submitted online within 14 calendar days of citation issuance.

What are common reasons for unsuccessful appeals?
Lack of parking rules knowledge; improper permit display; failure to purchase required permits; inclement weather; late arrival; parking in same area without prior ticket; parking in accessible space without disability placard; short parking duration; advice from others to park in particular lot.

How many citations are issued each year?
Approximately 50,000 citations annually.

Special Events

Where should I park during a home football game?
Pay-to-park lots on main and south campus east of Asp Avenue cost $30 per vehicle. Lloyd Noble Center lot offers parking with $5 roundtrip CART shuttle to stadium.

Where is disability parking during a home football game?
Asp Avenue Parking Facility provides disability parking with advance permit sales. Call (405) 325-3311. Law Center also offers CART paratransit van access to stadium's west side.

Where is RV parking during a home football game?
Lloyd Noble Center offers "SooneRVillage" RV parking; visit the website or call (405) 325-4666 for details.

Six lesser-known places to study during finals week at OU

If you spent dead week living at the Bizzell Memorial Library or in the dorm study lounges, check out some lesser-known study spots on campus to spice up your finals week studying.

1. Beaird Lounge (second floor, Oklahoma Memorial Union)

- Noise level: Fairly quiet except for the occasional student walking by.
- Nearest caffeine: Starbucks on the first floor of the union.
- Overall atmosphere: It has a living-room feel with nice, natural lighting, comfortable seating and books.
- Bonus: There is a fireplace and a Christmas tree, and it's open 24 hours per day.

2. Couches in Richards Hall (first floor)

- Noise level: Great for those who study better with ambient background noise.
- Nearest caffeine: The Bookmark Cafe in the Bizzell Memorial Library.
- Overall atmosphere: The leather couches are cozy, and it is just quiet enough.
- Bonus: There is a portable room containing radioactive material down the hall.

3. Keith Carroll Student-Faculty Lounge (third floor, Nielsen Hall)

- Noise level: Aside from the humming of a nearby heater, it stays quiet.
- Nearest caffeine: The Bookmark Cafe in the Bizzell Memorial Library.
- Overall atmosphere: With a chalkboard and tables, it's perfect for study groups.
- Bonus: The physics diagrams on the walls make one feel as if Albert Einstein studied here.

4. Swinging pendulum room (first floor, Nielsen Hall)

- Noise level: It is completely silent.
- Nearest caffeine: The Bookmark Cafe in the Bizzell Memorial Library.
- Overall atmosphere: It is comfortable and quiet, and the pendulum is somewhat soothing.
- Bonus: The ceiling is three stories high, a whole wall is made up of windows, and there is a swinging pendulum to watch on study breaks.

5. Architecture Lounge (first floor, Gould Hall)

- Noise level: It's quiet aside from the sounds of students walking by.
- Nearest caffeine: About equidistant to The Bookmark Cafe in the Bizzell Memorial Library and Roscoe's in Cate Restaurants.
- Overall atmosphere: With stone floors and modern furniture, it has a more contemporary layout than the interior of most campus buildings.
- Bonus: The lighting is just right for reading: neither too bright nor too dim.

6. The Nancy Mergler Library (first floor of David L. Boren Hall, Honors College)

- Noise level: Shhhh! It is a library!
- Nearest caffeine: Roscoe's in Cate Restaurants.
- Overall atmosphere: It is as cozy as a library should be, and there is plenty of natural light.
- Bonus: There are copies of various magazines and newspapers, including The Wall Street Journal, The Economist and The New Yorker.

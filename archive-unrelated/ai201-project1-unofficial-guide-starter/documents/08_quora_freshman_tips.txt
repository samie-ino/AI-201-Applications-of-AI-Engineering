Get to know your RA - they can be a great help or a big annoyance in your dorm (I was one so I know). Don't drink in your dorm room. If you're gonna drink, go off campus.

Also, get involved on campus. Take advantage of the free events on campus - free movies at Meacham, foam party, carnival on the South Oval, etc.

Take a nap every once in a while. Workout.

Have more cafe meals and less points on campus - it's a better deal and more filling. Find an upperclassmen and trade a trip to the cafe with a homecooked meal.

When you do your laundry in the dorm, set a timer on your phone and do homework while you're waiting.

Figure out if you're a morning person, like to knock out all your classes at once, or need frequent breaks to focus.

Write a study guide for each day - for example on Monday, read ch 3 for bio and ch 5 for history, tuesday - write outline for biology quiz, etc. That way, you're not cramming or burn out in marathon-long study sessions. I figured this out my final year of college and wish I did it sooner.

Have a planner and use it.

Go out for ihop pancakes or canes at midnight with friends - this is where some of the best memories are made.

Take time to hang out with your best friends - bfs and gfs may come/go or even stay, but you won't always have your bffs around post-college.

Take classes that are widely applicable but going to office hours will help you narrow down major. Go to office hours, period. Your professors are awesome people - lecture halls don't let you see that.

I absolutely loved my time at OU - ask me questions if you have them.
